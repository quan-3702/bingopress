BingoPress – How to use

Print bingo cards and call the game on your screen.

1. Choose the kind of bingo

  • Numbers 1–75: classic American bingo. The columns are B, I, N, G and O, and the middle square is FREE.
  • Word bingo: each square has a word or short phrase instead of a number. Great for baby showers, birthdays, holidays, classrooms and road trips.
  • Numbers 1–90 (Pro): British bingo tickets with 3 rows of 9 squares and 15 numbers on each ticket.

2. Word bingo

  1. Type your words in the big box, one on each line. You can also paste a list.
  2. Or pick a Ready-made list: Baby shower, Christmas, Sight words or Road trip.
  3. Choose the Card size. A 5 × 5 card needs at least 24 words, 4 × 4 needs 16 and 3 × 3 needs 9.
Type a few more words than you need. Then the cards are more different from each other and the game lasts longer.

3. Make the cards

  1. Type How many cards you need – one for each player, or two if people like to play two cards.
  2. Choose 1, 2 or 4 cards on each page. One card per page gives the biggest numbers for people who see less well.
  3. Type a Heading such as St. Mary's Bingo Night, pick a Colour and your Paper.
  4. Click 🖨 Print cards. Set Scale to 100% and turn on Background graphics for the colours.

Every card is different and has a number (Card 001, Card 002 …) and a set number. New set of cards makes a completely new set – only do this when you print new cards.

4. Calling slips and checklist

Under Also print you can add:

  • Calling slips – every number (or word) on its own slip. Cut them out, fold them and draw them from a bowl or hat.
  • Caller's checklist – a sheet with all the numbers, to cross off as you call them.

5. Call the game on the screen

  1. Click 🎱 Call the game.
  2. Click Call next or press the Space bar. The number appears in a big ball and is marked on the board.
  3. Tick Read each call aloud and your PC says each number, for example “B, 12”.
  4. Undo takes back the last call. New game clears the board but keeps the same cards.
Connect your PC to a TV or projector so everybody can see the numbers.

6. Check a winner

When somebody calls “Bingo!”, ask for the number printed under their card. Type it in Check a winning card and click Check. BingoPress shows the card with the called squares in orange and tells you if there is a full row, column, diagonal, four corners or a full card. For 90-ball tickets it shows one line, two lines or a full house.

7. Free version and Pro

The free version never limits how often you print or call.

  • Free: 75-ball and word bingo, up to 8 cards at a time, calling slips, checklist and the caller screen.
  • Pro (one-time purchase): up to 500 different cards at once and 90-ball tickets.

8. If something goes wrong

  • The colours are missing: turn on Background graphics in the print window.
  • Card check says “not yet” but the player has bingo: make sure the set number on their card is the same as the set number in BingoPress.
  • No sound: check that Read each call aloud is ticked and the PC volume is up.
