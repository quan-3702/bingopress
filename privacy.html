BingoPress – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

BingoPress works entirely on your device. Your settings, word lists and called numbers are stored locally on your PC and are never uploaded to any server.

Printed cards are created on your PC. When "Read each call aloud" is on, the text is spoken by the voices built into Windows.

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
